Meals & Dining

We provide three healthy meals a day for all residential students. Our menu is designed to be nutritious and culturally diverse to cater to our international student body.

Dining Schedule
Meal Time Notes
Breakfast 07:00 AM - 08:00 AM Tea, Porridge, Bread/Escort
Lunch 01:00 PM - 02:00 PM Posho, Rice, Beans, Peas, G.Nuts
Dinner 07:00 PM - 08:00 PM Rice, Matooke, Sweet Potatoes, Beef (Fridays)
Dining Hall Policy

All meals must be consumed within the Dining Hall. Taking utensils or food to the dormitories is strictly prohibited to maintain hygiene.
