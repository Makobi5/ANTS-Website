About ANTS

All Nations Theological College and Seminary (ANTS) is a Tertiary Institution registered by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) in Uganda. Founded in January 1993 as a joint initiative of East African (Ugandan), Korean, and American Christians, ANTS was established with a singular purpose — to train committed believers as pastors, church leaders, and missionaries for the growth and maturity of the Church across Africa and the world.
🇺🇬 Uganda 🇰🇷 Korea 🇺🇸 United States 📅 Est. January 1993 🎓 NCHE Accredited
