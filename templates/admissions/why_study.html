Why Study at ANTS?

Discover what makes All Nations Theological College & Seminary the right place to grow your faith, sharpen your mind, and answer your calling.

Share Tweet WhatsApp
ANTS is dedicated to preparing students at post-secondary level through rigorous instruction in the Bible and Theology, and through intentional training in character and spiritual formation — raising leaders who are ready not just academically, but for life and ministry.
🏛️
Fully Registered & Accredited

ANTS is recognised by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE), ensuring your academic credentials are valid nationally and internationally. When you enroll at ANTS, you join an institution that meets the highest national standards for tertiary education.

✝️
Christ-Centered Curriculum

Our curriculum is rooted in the unshakeable truth of Scripture, blending academic rigour with practical ministry formation. Whether you are called to pastoral ministry, missions, or counselling, our programmes are designed to deepen your faith and sharpen your mind.

💰
Affordable Education & Scholarships

We believe theological education should be accessible to all who are called. ANTS offers highly competitive tuition rates alongside bursaries and work-study programmes for students who demonstrate financial need and academic potential.

🌱
Holistic Student Development

At ANTS, transformation goes beyond the classroom. Our serene campus in Kisubi fosters spiritual reflection, community life, daily chapel services, and mentorship from experienced, godly faculty who invest personally in every student.

📚
Resourceful Ilam Library

The Ilam Library is stocked with an extensive collection of theological texts, Bible commentaries, and online academic journals. It provides a quiet, reflective environment designed to support deep study, serious research, and spiritual enquiry.

🌍
Diverse International Community

Study alongside believers from Uganda, Kenya, South Sudan, DR Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania, Ethiopia and beyond. The ANTS community is a rich tapestry of cultures and callings, preparing you for cross-cultural ministry in a globalised world.

🎓
Proven Track Record

Since 1993, ANTS has successfully held 28+ graduation ceremonies, sending out generations of pastors, missionaries, and church leaders who are actively serving across East Africa and the wider world.
