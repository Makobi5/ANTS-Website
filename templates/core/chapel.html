Spiritual Life & Worship

The Heart of Campus

A Place of Spiritual Formation

"They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer." — Acts 2:42

Completed in 2003, the ANTS Chapel has served as the spiritual centre of our community for over two decades. It is more than a building — it is where faculty and students gather daily to seek God's face, aligning academic pursuit with deep spiritual vitality.

Our worship is vibrant and interdenominational, reflecting the diverse cultures of the 10+ nations represented at ANTS. Through Morning Devotions, Community Chapel, and Evening Prayer, we mold servant leaders who are spiritually nourished and ready for ministry.

Spiritual Leadership

Office of the Chaplain

Welcome to the ANTS Chapel — a sacred space set apart for encounter, renewal, and growth. Whether you are a new student finding your footing in faith, a seasoned believer deepening your walk, or simply someone seeking God's presence, this Chapel is for you.

My role as Chaplain is to walk alongside every member of our community — in seasons of joy, in moments of doubt, and in the ordinary rhythm of daily life. I believe that spiritual formation is not a destination but a daily journey, and it is my privilege to journey with you.

I warmly invite you to be part of our chapel programmes — from Morning Devotions to Friday Evening Prayer. Come as you are. Leave strengthened, encouraged, and equipped for God's purpose in your life.

Dushimeyesu Ismael Chaplain, ANTS
