Welcome Home, Alumni!

Alumni will be part of a community that offers various benefits including:

  • A regular e-newsletter with updates on the College.
  • Ability to connect and network with fellow Alumni.
  • Invitation to University events including public lectures and re-unions.
  • Access to the university library on site free of charge.
  • Networking opportunities.
  • Opportunity to share your knowledge experience to inspire others.

How to Get Involved

  1. Support students' employability through offering internships.
  2. Connect with fellow alumni by attending events.
  3. Help the College in organizing alumni reunions.
  4. Keep us informed of your accomplishments and awards.
  5. Make a donation to support College research.
  6. Speak to current students at networking receptions.
