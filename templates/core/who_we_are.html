Our History

ANTS was founded in 1993 with a clear and urgent vision: to equip believers across Uganda who lacked formal theological training. Opening its doors with just 17 students and 6 lecturers, the college offered a 2-year Diploma in Theology. Over the decades, ANTS has grown from a modest classroom into a fully accredited tertiary institution drawing students from across East Africa.

Our Philosophy

"From Learning to Ministry." At ANTS, we believe in Head, Heart, and Hands training. It is not enough to acquire knowledge intellectually — students must be transformed spiritually and equipped practically for service. We are driven to inspire students to apply what they learn directly into church ministries as they serve God.

Uganda needs well-nourished, spiritually grounded people who are able to contribute honestly to the education and evangelisation of the nation. ANTS is uniquely designed to build confident church leaders by combining spirituality and academics — producing well-balanced ministers ready for the Gospel.
