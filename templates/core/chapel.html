Spiritual Life & Worship

The Heart of Campus

A Place of Spiritual Formation

"They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship..." - Acts 2:42

Since its establishment in 2004, the ANTS Chapel has served as the spiritual center of our community. It is more than just a building; it is where faculty and students gather daily to seek God's face, aligning our academic pursuit with spiritual vitality.

Our worship style is vibrant and interdenominational, reflecting the diverse cultures of the 10+ nations represented at ANTS. Whether through Morning Glory, Community Chapel, or Evening Devotions, our goal is to mold servant leaders who are spiritually nourished.
